Privacy Policy

Information requested

The website requests information used to arrange experiences and respond to inquiries, including giver and recipient contact details, personal messages, city and date preferences, payment references, and private-experience inquiry details.

How information is used

Information submitted to Anion is used to respond to the request, arrange the invitation and experience, and match payment details. Please provide recipient information only when you are authorized to share it, and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information.

Mystery giving

The mystery-giving option requests that the giver’s identity be withheld from the recipient. Anion still needs the giver’s information to arrange the experience. A personal message may reveal the giver’s identity.

Payment and external services

Payment takes place on PayPal’s website, under PayPal’s privacy practices. This page does not collect card details. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the browser requests needed to serve them.

Browser storage and previews

The website code uses session storage for campaign-source information. The invitation preview temporarily holds a companion name in page memory; it clears that value when the preview closes and does not submit it as a recipient selection.

Forms and analytics

Form delivery depends on a configured submission service or email destination. The page reports when a form is not connected. Analytics collection depends on whether an analytics ID has been configured.
